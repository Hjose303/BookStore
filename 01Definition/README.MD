We need a system that allows managing the books available in a bookstore, registering customers, and recording sales, with the aim of maintaining proper control of the books, their stock, prices, and sales transactions. The system should also help monitor the bookstore's income and provide basic economic reports for a specific period.
To understand what the program must accomplish, it is necessary to consider the main information related to books, including their authors, publishers, genres, categories, subject areas, educational levels, prices, and available stock. For example, a physics book may be intended for high school students, while another physics book may be designed for university students. The system must allow books to be classified according to these characteristics to make searching and organizing them easier.
The system will remain simple and suitable for a small bookstore. Its main functions will focus on book management through CRUD operations, customer registration, book searches by title, author, price, or publisher, stock control, sales recording, invoice generation, and sales reports for a selected period. It will also provide reports on the books sold, inventory levels, and the bookstore's income and expenses to support basic business management.
