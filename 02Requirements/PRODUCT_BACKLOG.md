# **Overview**



This document outlines the Product Backlog for the Bookly desktop application developed by 404 Founders - Team 5. The backlog categorizes functional requirements by priority to ensure core inventory management and sales operations are established first.



# **1. High-Priority Items (Core Functionality)**



## **PBI-01: Book Registration**



**Description:** The system must allow users to register new books into the database, capturing fundamental details such as title, author, ISBN, category, unit price, and initial stock level.



**Story Points:** 5



**Status:** To Do



## **PBI-02: Book Information Management**



**Description:** To maintain database integrity, the system must provide CRUD capabilities allowing users to view, edit, and delete existing book records as catalog details or pricing change over time.



**Story Points:** 5



**Status:** To Do



## **PBI-03: Inventory Management**



**Description:** The application must actively control available stock, automatically updating inventory counts whenever new shipments arrive or when books are sold, ensuring accurate store availability at all times.



**Story Points:** 8



**Status:** To Do



## **PBI-04: Sales Registration**



**Description:** The system must enable users to process customer transactions by selecting one or more books, recording the date of sale, and specifying the quantities purchased.



**Story Points:** 8



**Status:** To Do



# **2. Medium-Priority Items (Transaction Processing \& Search)**



## **PBI-05: Calculate Sale Total**



**Description:** During checkout, the system must automatically calculate the total cost of a transaction by multiplying individual item prices by quantities ordered and summing up the subtotals.



**Story Points:** 3



**Status:** To Do



## **PBI-06: Generate Invoice**



**Description:** Upon completing a sale, the application must generate a readable invoice or proof of purchase containing transaction details, itemized totals, and purchase dates.



**Story Points:** 5



**Status:** To Do



## **PBI-08: Book Search and Consultation**



**Description:** To help store clerks assist customers quickly, the system must include a search feature allowing queries by title, author, category, or ISBN.



**Story Points:** 3



**Status:** To Do



# **3. Low-Priority Items (Reporting \& Customer Tracking)**



## **PBI-07: Sales Reports**



**Description:** The system must offer reporting features that aggregate sales and revenue data over specified time periods (e.g., daily, monthly, or yearly) to help track financial growth.



**Story Points:** 5



**Status:** To Do



## **PBI-09: Report of Sold Books**



**Description:** The application must generate top-seller reports, allowing store managers to analyze book popularity, assess inventory turnover, and make informed restocking decisions.



**Story Points:** 5



**Status:** To Do



## **PBI-10: Customer Registration**



**Description:** The system must allow users to store basic customer profile information, such as full names and email addresses, to facilitate smoother invoice generation and customer record-keeping.



**Story Points:** 3



**Status:** To Do



## **PBI-11: Personalized recommendations**



**Description:** The system must generate book recommendations based on the customer's registered preferences and purchase history.



**Story Points:** 8



**Status:** To Do



## **PBI-12: Recommendation email**



**Description:** The system must allow personalized book recommendations to be sent to the customer's registered email address.



**Story Points:** 8



**Status:** To Do
