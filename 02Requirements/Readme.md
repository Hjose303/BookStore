In the context of a bookstore it’s necessary to keep a record of the books available for sale, including information such as the title, author, category , price, and available quantity.
When a customer buys one or more books, the sale must be recorded in the system. Based on this information, the system should automatically update the stock, calculate the total value of the sale, and generate an invoice or proof of purchase. Additionally, the system will allow you to check information about the books and sales made.
The system will also allow you analyze sales through reports by time periods and check the income earned over a specific period.

Main features
1. CRUD BOOK ------> Create, Read, Update, Delete BOOKS
2. searchBook ------> Search for books by author, title, price, or category.
3. recordSales ------> Record the books sold, the quantity, and automatically calculate the total.
4. generateInvoice ------> Generate a receipt for the sale made.
5. checkStock ------> View the number of copies available for each book.
6. Sales report ------> Check the sales made within a specific period of time.
7. Low stock alert ------> Identify the books whose available quantity is below the established minimum stock.
8. Sales of the day ------> Show a summary of the sales made during the day.
9. Sales history ------> Check previous sales along with the information from your receipts.

In general terms
The operation of the system can be summed up as follows:
Register books → Check stock → Register sale → Update stock → Generate invoice → Generate reports
